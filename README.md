## 🌐 SaaS / Hosted Platforms

> **Market Overview:** The global Integration Platform as a Service (iPaaS) market is estimated at **$13.9B – $19.2B in 2026** (projected to exceed $100B by 2034–2035 at a 24%–34% CAGR). The sector is **moderately concentrated**, with the top 5 vendors commanding roughly 45%–52% of total market revenue while competing against specialized low-code tools and hyperscaler native integration suites.
